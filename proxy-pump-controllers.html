Proxy Pump Controllers

The intelligence layer behind every PROXYS pump.

Proxy Pump Controllers are MPPT-based solar controllers that sit between your PV array and your PROXYS-PUMP motor — tracking the panel's maximum power point through the day, protecting the motor from common fault conditions, and giving you a clear digital readout of how the system is running.

MPPT Tracking

Maximises output from your solar panel array.

Digital Display

Clear, at-a-glance system readout.

Dry-Run Protect

Guards the motor when the water source runs dry.

Motor Protection

Built to work alongside every PROXYS pump.

Features & Benefits

What the controller does for your system.

Automatic Pump Control

Starts and manages the pump automatically as solar conditions change through the day.

Motor Protection

Helps guard the connected motor against common fault conditions during operation.

Dry-Run Protection

Detects a dry source and steps in to help protect the pump from running without water.

Smart, MPPT-Based Operation

Tracks the panel array's maximum power point to make the most of available sunlight.

Easy Installation

Designed to be wired in between the PV array and the PROXYS-PUMP motor.

Energy Efficient Operation

Aims to draw the most usable output from your solar panels, not just the pump's rated load.

Digital Display

Reliable Performance
