Surface Pumps

Monoblock pumps built for powerful, everyday water lifting.

The PROXYS-PUMP PG Series is a range of surface monoblock pumps designed for straightforward, dependable water lifting — from household overhead tanks to field-edge irrigation and small workshop use. Each pump pairs a robust cast motor housing with a wide suction and delivery bore for strong, consistent flow.

Features & Benefits

Built for the way these pumps actually get used.

Solar DC & AC Motors

Three DC models (PG0110DC, PG01210DC, PG01310DC) built for solar arrays, plus an AC model (PG0510AC) for grid-powered installations.

Robust Monoblock Body

A single-piece cast motor-and-pump housing keeps alignment tight and reduces the leak points common in coupled setups.

Wide Suction & Delivery Bore

Bore sizes scale from 2" up to 4" across the range, supporting higher discharge without excess strain on the motor.

High Discharge Capacity

From 99,000 LPD on the entry PG0110DC up to 5,28,000 LPD on the flagship PG0510AC.
