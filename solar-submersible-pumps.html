Solar Submersible Pumps

Deep-well irrigation, powered entirely by the sun.

PROXYS-PUMP solar submersible pumps are stainless-steel bodied borewell pumps built for deep agricultural wells. Paired with a PV array and our Proxy Pump Controller, they run without grid electricity or diesel — supporting India's PM-KUSUM solar agriculture mission, farmer camp by farmer camp, across Uttar Pradesh.
