Who We Are

A Noida-Based Manufacturer, Out In The Fields With Farmers.

PROXYS Solar Pump is the solar water pump brand of Parkash Gold Ind Pvt Ltd, based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Rather than selling only through dealers, our team runs direct outreach camps in villages and government agricultural centres — from Mudiya Pamar to the State Level Agricultural Training Centre in Bulandshahr — helping farmers understand and apply for the PM-KUSUM solar pump subsidy.

Ask Us Directly
What is the PM-KUSUM subsidy on solar pumps?

PM-KUSUM is a central government scheme that supports farmers in installing solar-powered irrigation pumps with a subsidy, reducing the upfront cost of moving away from diesel or grid-dependent pumping.

How do I apply for the scheme?

Applications are submitted through the official Uttar Pradesh Agriculture Department portal. Our field teams assist farmers with documentation at our village and government-centre camps.

What pump capacities does PROXYS manufacture?

Our range spans from smaller domestic monoblock pumps to larger agricultural submersible pumps, along with matching solar panels and controllers.

Does PROXYS provide after-sales support?

Yes — maintenance, repair and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) are available, along with a toll-free helpline for support requests.

Can I see a live installation before buying?

Yes, our team can arrange a site visit, and our farmer camps regularly demonstrate working solar pump setups in the field.
